# Refund Policy

## Rain and Weather Policy (T20 specific)

For matches affected by rain or wet venue conditions, the following rules apply:
- **Full Refund:** If no ball is bowled during the booked slot due to rain or unplayable wet venue conditions, the entire venue fee will be refunded.
- **No Refund:** If even a single ball is bowled and the match is subsequently interrupted or cancelled due to rain, no refund will be issued. The booking is considered utilized once the match commences.
- The venue owner or on-site staff have the final authority to decide if the venue is playable.

For T20 (20 overs), the refund rules for rain are:

| Situation | Refund % | Example (₹10,000 Match) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No toss yet | 100% | ₹10,000 |
| 1st innings < 6 overs | 75% | ₹7,500 |
| 1st innings 6-20 overs | 50% | ₹5,000 |
| 1st innings complete, 2nd innings < 6 overs | 25% | ₹2,500 |
| 2nd innings 6+ overs bowled | 0% (match done) | ₹0 |

*Note: Refunds are calculated on the base ground price. Platform and convenience fees are non-refundable once the match is booked.*

The decision on whether a venue is playable rests with the venue owner or site staff.
